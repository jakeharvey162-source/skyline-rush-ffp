# Skyline Rush: Rooftop Strike — Poki submission notes

## Positioning

A lightweight browser-first FPS set above a fictional Johannesburg-inspired skyline. Players clear three escalating Rogue Machine waves, chaining quick eliminations to increase a Rush multiplier and chase a better score.

## Why this web version is distinct

- Built as a browser-native edition instead of wrapping the Windows executable.
- Immediate play with a tiny runtime build.
- Mouse/keyboard and touch controls.
- Short repeatable runs designed around Poki session behavior.
- African-inspired Skyline Rush identity without external IP.
- No email login, no chat, no third-party tracking or third-party ads.

## Suggested audience/categories

Use Poki's closest current categories to:
- Shooting
- Action
- Skill

Do not market it as online multiplayer until the Poki edition actually has approved multiplayer.

## Monetization

Implemented through Poki SDK only:
- commercialBreak at natural replay/resume points
- optional rewardedBreak revive after defeat

There are no in-app purchases and no reward wall.

## Testing target

Aim for:
- 65%+ conversion to play
- 5+ minute average playtime

Those are targets from Poki's current testing guidance, not guaranteed outcomes. Use the first 10 recorded playtests and the 500-player fit test to tune wave difficulty, onboarding, and replay hooks.
